# Google Business Profile Checklist — Obey One Way Taxi

Keep the local ranking edge. Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single biggest lever for "taxi near me" searches. Do these on a recurring schedule.

## Setup (one time)
- [ ] Claim the listing at https://business.google.com — search "Obey One Way Taxi Chennai"
- [ ] Business name exactly: `Obey One Way Taxi` (no keywords stuffed in the name)
- [ ] Category: `Taxi service` (primary). Add secondary: `Limo service`, `Airport shuttle service`, `Transportation service`
- [ ] Address: Chennai, Tamil Nadu (match the site's `siteConfig.address`)
- [ ] Phone: `+91 86672 19259` — identical to website footer, robots/schema NAP
- [ ] Website: `https://obeyonewaytaxi.com`
- [ ] Hours: Open 24 hours, 7 days (matches `openingHours` `Mo-Su 00:00-23:59`)
- [ ] Services list: One Way Taxi, Drop Taxi, Airport Transfer, Outstation Cab, Round Trip, One Way Car Rental
- [ ] Service area: add the 9 city pages' cities (Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Trichy, Salem, Erode, Vellore, Pondicherry, Bangalore)
- [ ] Attributes: `Mobile payments accepted` (if true), `Free Wi-Fi`, `Verified`

## Posting (weekly)
- [ ] Photo post: a real cab photo (rooftop, interior) — tagged with route, e.g. "Chennai → Coimbatore"
- [ ] Offer post: e.g. "Flat fare to airport drop — book via WhatsApp"
- [ ] Update post: new city or route added, or new blog post published
- [ ] Video post (every 2 weeks): 30-sec dash-cam / driver intro / airport pickup walkthrough
- [ ] Question post: "Which is your favourite outstation route from Chennai?"

## Reviews (weekly)
- [ ] Ask every completed booking for a Google review (site already has the review-collection CTA)
- [ ] Reply to every review within 48h — thank + mention the route/cab
- [ ] Never ask for paid or incentivised reviews (violates Google policy, risks removal)

## Consistency
- [ ] NAP (Name/Address/Phone) identical on: GBP, website, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Facebook, Instagram
- [ ] Add GBP link to the footer once you have the review URL (`siteConfig.googleReviewUrl`)
- [ ] Verify ownership monthly; respond to Google's "improve your profile" prompts

## Metrics (monthly)
- [ ] Track: search views, map views, calls, direction requests, website clicks
- [ ] Target: 20+ reviews in 3 months, >50 in 6 months, 4.5+ average
