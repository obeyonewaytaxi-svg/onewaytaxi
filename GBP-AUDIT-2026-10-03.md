## Three real problems found

### 1. The review link was dead — this is why you have only 9 reviews

`src/config/site.ts` shipped `https://g.page/r/2sI1MGqBqn0/review`, and Google's own
"Review on Google" button used it. That link:

```
302  -> https://g.page/r/2sI1MGqBqn0/review/
302  -> https://www.google.com          <-- generic homepage, not a review form
```

Every customer you sent to leave a review landed on the Google homepage. **The link
collected nothing.** This is the single highest-impact bug found in this audit.

**Fixed** to Google's own review-form endpoint for your place ID:
`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJcxbGsRr3UjoRfaqBajA1wto`

Verified: signed-out visitors are now sent to sign in and then **returned to the review
form**, which the old link never did.

### 2. Your site said "Chennai"; your listing is in Potheri

The site's structured data claimed `addressLocality: Chennai`. The profile is registered at
**Potheri, Kattankulathur, PIN 603203** — about 45 km south, near SRM University.

Mismatched NAP between site and profile weakens exactly the local signal your GBP provides.
**Fixed** — schema now mirrors the listing (street, locality, PIN), so Google can
cross-reference them.

### 3. The listing is hard to find by name

Searching Google Maps for `Obey One Way Taxi Chennai` returns **ten other businesses** —
Silver One Way Taxi, Cab Service, Nathan Travels, Vip One Way Taxi — and **not yours.** The
listing only surfaces when searching by website URL.

This is almost certainly the Chennai/Potheri mismatch plus a sparse profile. Fixes:
- Set **service areas** to the 9 cities (you serve Chennai; you're located near Vellore)
- Add **10+ real photos** — the listing currently shows none
- **Reply to all 9 reviews** (30%+ reply rate is a ranking signal)
- Add **service list**: One Way Taxi, Drop Taxi, Airport Transfer, Outstation, Round Trip

⚠️ One thing to check yourself: the payload has a bare `5` before the review strings. I
could not confirm whether that is a **5.0 rating** or a field offset, and I did not want to
invent a rating. Your dashboard shows the real number.
